What they produce

Summaries computed from the simulations' counts (first draw, strata summed), not their settings: epidemic shape, revisions and observation noise. The delays have their own section below.

Delay structure

How the reporting delays are made, from each simulation's counts (first draw): the delay distribution, how it moves over the series, its weekday effects, how much strata differ and the release cadence with the wait it adds; then how strata's weekly delays move together, how the delay follows the log daily count, over-reporting withdrawn later and the share of the delay's weekly variation common to strata, fixed by stratum or by stratum and week. The deep dive shows these stratum by stratum. Delays are of reference dates at least the 95% delay before the last report, so the series' end does not shorten them; a release is a report date with any count. Dashed lines are the same summaries of the German development half (states) and the BDBV hub's releases (provinces), from the workflow's delay_reference rule.
